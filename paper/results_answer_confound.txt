Reading:
- CLEVR: exception-specific forgetting under replay (ER, DER++) holds within both answers. Robust.
- Constructed tasks: within-answer gaps are small (about -0.01) for DER++ and LwF; ~0 for ER.
- GQA and CLOVE (existence tables): the aggregate gap is mostly an answer-class effect. Within the same answer,
  exceptions are not forgotten more (DER++ on GQA "yes" cells is the one small exception, -0.03).
- So the pre-registered PASSes of M1 (GQA part), C1 and V1 do not show a structure effect beyond answer class.
- The rule+exceptions buffer still helps within answer (constructed tasks, CLEVR), but on existence tables its
  exception cells are mostly "yes", so part of its gain there may be class rebalancing; a class-balanced random
  buffer control has not been run.
- VLM: forgetting falls on "yes" cells in general (rule-yes -0.054, exception-yes -0.072 under finetune), not on
  exceptions as such. Also: fine-tuning on these tables lowers exception accuracy below zero-shot (0.82 -> 0.66-0.75)
  while raising rule accuracy (0.93 -> 0.96).
