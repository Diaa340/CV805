- POST-HOC, why the gate failed: the model learns the new words for the rule cells (zero-shot 0.644 -> task-only
  0.861) but not for the exceptions, which get WORSE with training (0.530 -> 0.441; exception "no" cells 0.746 ->
  0.278). The pilot (+0.16 to +0.26) used natural test samples (~10% exceptions); the gate used the 50/50 sample.
- So, when a VLM must learn new word-image bindings within this budget, LoRA learns the additive rule and overrides
  the exceptions (below chance). The bottleneck is acquisition, not retention: exceptions that are never learned
  cannot be forgotten. This matches the forward-transfer finding (exceptions are learned worse) and the small models
  on GQA / CLOVE, where exceptions also sit near chance after training.
